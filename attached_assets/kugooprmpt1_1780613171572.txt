KUGOO ECOSYSTEM - MASTER PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRODUCTION VERSION)

MISSION

Build Kugoo as a production-grade, investor-ready food delivery ecosystem, not as a prototype.

Every feature, database design, API, workflow, security implementation, user experience, animation, AI interaction, payment system, and infrastructure decision must be designed for scalability, maintainability, performance, security, App Store compliance, Google Play compliance, and future expansion.

The final product should feel comparable to Uber Eats, DoorDash, Glovo, Bolt Food, and Deliveroo while maintaining a unique Kugoo identity.

ORDER FLOW

Customer places order.

Vendor receives notification.

Vendor accepts order.

Vendor begins preparing food.

Vendor marks:

"Food Ready"

System automatically finds nearest available rider.

Available rider receives delivery request.

Rider accepts.

Rider travels to vendor.

Rider confirms pickup.

Customer sees live tracking.

Rider delivers order.

Customer clicks:

"Order Received"

Rider clicks:

"Delivered"

Order closes successfully.
